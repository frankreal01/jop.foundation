01 / WHO WE ARE

A foundation built around becoming.

JOP Foundation is a purpose-driven platform focused on helping young people become more intentional about who they are, what they develop and how they contribute.

We believe potential is only a beginning. People need direction, self-awareness, useful skills, disciplined habits and environments that encourage growth. Our work is therefore not simply about telling young people to “do more”; it is about helping them build the foundations required to do meaningful things well.

02 / WHY WE EXIST

JOP / 002

Potential needs direction.

Young people carry enormous potential. But potential alone does not determine what someone becomes.

Direction matters. Development matters. Environment matters. The choices a person makes repeatedly matter. JOP Foundation exists to create conversations, learning experiences and opportunities that help young people move from possibility toward purposeful development.

We are not trying to tell people who to become. We are helping them become more intentional about it.

07 / THE FOUNDER

Meet the person behind the purpose.

JOP Foundation began from a conviction that young people need more than potential. They need the direction and development that help potential become capacity.

Julius Franklin is building JOP Foundation around a simple belief: how we understand ourselves influences how we act, what we build and what we contribute. The Foundation is an attempt to turn that belief into a platform for purposeful development.

“I believe that who we become should be intentional — and that our lives should eventually show evidence of what we value.”
